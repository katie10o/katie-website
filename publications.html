CSCW · 2026 · Peer-reviewed paper

PRISM: Evaluating a Rule-Based, Scenario-Driven Social Media Privacy Education Program for Young Autistic Adults

Kirsten Chapman, Garrett Smith, Kaitlyn Klabacka, Joseph Thomas Bills, Addisyn Bushman, Terisa Gabrielsen, Pamela J. Wisniewski, Xinru Page

PRISM evaluates a 14-week, scenario-based social-media privacy curriculum designed for young autistic adults with substantial support needs. The intervention used structured, contextual rules to support safer privacy decisions.

CSCW · 2025 · Peer-reviewed paper

Persuasiveness of Conversational Agents for Targeted Advertising: Autism and Gen-AI Chatbots

Kirsten Chapman, Kaitlyn Klabacka, Garrett Smith, Xinru Page

This study examines responses to persuasive advertising delivered through generative-AI chatbots. It compares autistic adults with different support needs and non-autistic adults, highlighting elevated persuasion risks for autistic adults with substantial support needs.
